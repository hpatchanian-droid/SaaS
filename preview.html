Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to try LVL Ops? ▾
No. The Starter tier is free forever and there's a 14-day Growth trial without a card.
Can I import my existing data? ▾
Yes — we support CSV imports for products, customers, and invoices, plus an API for everything else.
Is my data secure? ▾
All connections are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Role-based permissions and audit logs are built in.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Of course. Plans are month-to-month and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
